What to do
1 · Pick the coinChoose only a contract with solid 24h turnover and a funding rate large enough to matter after futures fees.
2 · Follow the directionPositive funding → SHORT receives. Negative funding → LONG receives. Use the direction shown on the coin card.
3 · Size conservativelyStart with the selected trade amount, not the full $680. Keep the remainder as margin reserve to reduce liquidation risk.
4 · Exit if edge disappearsDo not hold just for funding if the rate flips, liquidity falls, or price risk becomes larger than expected funding income.
Funding is paid between traders, not charged by KuCoin as a funding service fee. KuCoin can dynamically change settlement intervals, including hourly settlement during extreme funding conditions, so the dashboard uses the live interval returned by KuCoin when available. This remains a paper/planning aid; an unhedged perpetual position can lose more from price movement than it earns from funding.
