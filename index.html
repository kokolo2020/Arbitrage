Launch Hunter starts from fresh KuCoin listing announcements and checks whether the same USDT token has live top-of-book prices on KuCoin, MEXC, Gate.io and OKX. It ranks the cheapest live ask against the highest live bid. This is a discovery/paper tool, not executable arbitrage: withdrawal/deposit status, chain compatibility, transfer time, order-book depth, token restrictions and launch volatility must be verified before any real trade. ICO/launch investing can lose most or all of the allocation.
